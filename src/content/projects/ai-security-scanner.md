Early stage. Currently a specification and research notes rather than shipping code.

The premise is that AI startups ship fast, handle sensitive data, and rarely have a
security function in the first year. The product scans for the failure modes specific
to LLM applications, then sells continuous monitoring rather than a point-in-time
report.
